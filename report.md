## Method

Audio from the municipality's own YouTube channel. Transcription with faster-whisper large-v3-turbo (Dutch, VAD, word timestamps). Speaker turns from sherpa-onnx (pyannote segmentation 3.0 and 3D-Speaker embeddings). Agenda and vote counts from the Lokaal Beslist harvester SPARQL endpoints where the municipality is harvested. A Gemini reading of the transcript says where each item is announced, and those starts are kept as anchors; the remaining items are placed by a monotone alignment of the agenda titles to 45-second transcript windows (multilingual MiniLM embeddings plus spoken cues); for municipalities without an official agenda the Gemini reading is the only source. Summaries by Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite, verified: a quote must exist in the transcript, and a person is named only when they are on the council list or in the agenda title.
